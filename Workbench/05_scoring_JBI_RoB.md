# JBI RoB scoring rules — no numeric sums

Sources: JBI Manual for Evidence Synthesis; Munn et al. prevalence guidance; Barker et al. revised cross-sectional tool; JBI cohort tool.
Two independent appraisers. Quote verbatim per item.

## Rules for all designs

- Per item: `Yes / No / Unclear / N/A` + quote + page.
- Overall per study: `Include / Exclude / Seek further info` + one-line reason.
- NEVER sum scores or set cutoffs (e.g. "≥7 = high quality"). JBI forbids it.
- Disagreements: discussion → third reviewer → contact authors if still unclear.

## Prevalence studies (9 items — most surveillance reports go here)

1. Sample frame appropriate to target population?
2. Participants sampled appropriately? (consecutive/random vs convenience/LBM-only — note it)
3. Sample size adequate? (calculation or large national survey justification; low-prevalence needs larger n)
4. Subjects + setting described in detail? (species, production type, state, period)
5. Coverage of identified sample in analysis? (dropouts/missing accounted for)
6. Valid methods for condition ID? (OIE/validated PCR/sequencing vs unvalidated rapid test)
7. Condition measured standardly/reliably for all? (same assay, trained collectors, inter-observer check)
8. Appropriate stats? (numerator + denominator + CI; correct method for design)
9. Response rate adequate / non-response managed? (compare responders vs non-responders)

## Analytical cross-sectional (8 items — risk-factor/association papers)

1. Inclusion criteria defined? 2. Subjects + setting detailed? 3. Exposure measured validly/reliably?
4. Objective standard condition criteria? 5. Confounders identified? 6. Confounder strategies stated?
7. Outcomes measured validly/reliably? 8. Appropriate stats (confounder handling visible)?

## Cohort (11 items — follow-up/outbreak cohorts)

Selection/allocation, exposure classification, confounding, temporal precedence (exposure before outcome?),
outcome assessment/detection, retention/follow-up completeness, stats. Same Yes/No/Unclear/N-A + overall rule.

## How RoB feeds synthesis (link to SWiM 6 + GRADE)

- Tabulate RoB per study; order synthesis tables by RoB where informative.
- Sensitivity: re-read key claims with high-RoB studies removed; report if conclusions change.
