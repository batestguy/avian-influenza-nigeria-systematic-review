# PRISMA-P scoring — protocol freeze (17 items / 26 sub-items)

Source: Shamseer et al., Syst Rev 2015 (PRISMA-P). Use BEFORE screening starts.
Score `Yes / Partial / No`. All Methods items must be `Yes` before Phase 2.

**Admin:** 1a protocol identified; 1b update?; 2 registration (PROSPERO name + number or "not registered"); 3a authors + roles; 3b guarantor; 4 amendments plan; 5 support/funding + roles.
**Intro:** 6 rationale (vs Kalonda + Akanbi); 7 objectives with CoCoPop/PEO.
**Methods:** 8 eligibility (study + report traits: design, setting, timeframe 2006–2026, language, status); 9 sources + coverage dates; 10 draft strategy for ≥1 database; 11a data management; 11b selection (reviewers, independence, stages); 11c extraction (pilot, duplicate, author contact); 12 data items/variables; 13 outcomes + prioritization; 14 RoB method + level + use in synthesis; 15a synthesis eligibility rule; 15b summary measures/consistency (or why N/A); 15c extra analyses (subgroup/sensitivity); 15d narrative summary plan if no meta-analysis; 16 meta-bias plan; 17 certainty (GRADE) plan.

Freeze rule: any change after screening starts = dated amendment + Methods note.
