## Frozen question (CoCoPop)

**Condition:** Avian influenza virus infection/detection, including HPAI and LPAI; subtype, clade/lineage/sublineage, reassortment, mutation and genetic diversity evidence.
**Context:** Nigeria, 1 January 2006 – 19 September 2026. Start = first recognised HPAI H5N1 epidemic; end = operational review search cutoff selected on 2026-09-19.
**Population:** Domestic poultry (commercial, backyard, live-bird markets), wild and migratory birds and wetland-associated birds, and virus isolates linked to Nigeria.

**Central question:** How have avian influenza viruses emerged, evolved genetically, and redistributed spatially and temporally in Nigeria from 2006 to 2026?

## Frozen objectives (4, map 1:1 to syntheses)

1. Reconstruct first detection and temporal distribution by subtype/clade.
2. Map geographic distribution by state/zone and epoch.
3. Characterise host range and host-system shifts (poultry systems vs wild birds).
4. Synthesise molecular evolution: clade replacement, lineage diversification, reassortment and mutation claims with evidence grading.

## Frozen synthesis groupings (SWiM Item 1)

- Epochs: 2006–2010 / 2011–2014 / 2015–2020 / 2021–2026.
- Virus: subtype (H5N1, H5N2, H5N6, H5N8, H9N2, other) × clade/lineage where reported.
- Host system: commercial / backyard / live-bird market / wild-migratory / wild-resident.
- Geography: state, then zone. Empty cells = `Not reported`, never blank.

## Tightened eligibility (replaces broad P27–28 prose on paste)

**Include if ALL true:** (a) Nigeria conducted OR Nigeria-specific data extractable; (b) HPAI and/or LPAI AIV in avian hosts with lab confirmation (molecular and/or serological with assay stated); (c) ≥1 of: epi occurrence/prevalence/outbreak/surveillance, molecular/phylogenetic result, spatial/temporal distribution, wild-bird finding; (d) primary research, surveillance, outbreak investigation or molecular-epidemiology report with extractable empirical data; (e) English full text, or English abstract + translatable full text with translation method logged.
**Exclude if ANY true:** entirely outside Nigeria with no Nigeria data; non-AIV only; no epi/molecular/spatial/temporal extractable data; editorial/commentary/news/conference abstract without data; lab-only experiment with no field relevance; duplicate dataset (keep most comprehensive; link others as companions).
**Report traits:** 2006–2026 publication with data in window; peer-reviewed plus defined grey (theses, WOAH/FDL reports) with sensitivity analysis; overlapping datasets resolved by completeness rule.
