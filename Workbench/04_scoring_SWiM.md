Source: Campbell et al., BMJ 2020;l6890. Use with PRISMA 2020 Item 13d.
Score `Yes / Partial / No`. No meta-analysis is expected here — SWiM IS the synthesis standard.

1. **Grouping** — how studies grouped per synthesis (epochs, subtype/clade, host system, zone/state) + rationale.
2. **Standardised metric** — per synthesis: first detection / occurrence / clade assignment; transformations stated.
3. **Synthesis method** — structured tabulation + narrative + ordering rule; why chosen; why no pooling.
4. **Prioritisation** — which results prioritised for summary and why.
5. **Heterogeneity** — how differences explored (surveillance intensity vs true emergence; assay differences).
6. **Certainty link** — how RoB/certainty fed into synthesis wording (no vote-counting on significance alone).
7. **Data presentation** — tables/plots specified, ordering key stated, every study referenced.
8. **Reporting results** — per comparison/outcome: synthesised finding + certainty + contributing studies; language matches question.
9. **Limitations of synthesis** — grouping/method limits + effect on conclusions.

Fail patterns to reject: "narrative synthesis was done" with no Items 1–4; vote-count by p-value; synthesis sentence with no table link.
