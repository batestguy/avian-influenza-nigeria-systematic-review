## Review title

Emergence, molecular evolution and spatiotemporal dynamics of avian influenza viruses in Nigeria, 2006–2026: a systematic review

## Rationale

Nigeria saw one of Africa's earliest HPAI H5N1 incursions (2006) with 2015 re-emergence and continued diversification including clade 2.3.4.4b and 2024 H9N2. Existing syntheses (Kalonda et al. 2020 sub-Saharan Africa; Akanbi & Lakes 2025 Nigeria broad narrative, PubMed+Scopus only, no flow/RoB/strings) leave the integrated temporal–geographic–host–clade/reassortment reconstruction unaddressed. This review provides it for surveillance and One Health policy.

## Objectives (CoCoPop)

Condition: AIV infection/detection (HPAI + LPAI; subtype, clade/lineage, reassortment, mutation, diversity). Context: Nigeria, 01 Jan 2006–30 Sep 2026. Population: domestic poultry (commercial, backyard, live-bird markets), wild/migratory/wetland birds, Nigeria-linked isolates. Question: How have AIVs emerged, evolved genetically, and redistributed spatially/temporally in Nigeria 2006–2026? Objectives: (1) first-detection/temporal; (2) geographic by state/zone/epoch; (3) host range/system shifts; (4) clade replacement, diversification, reassortment/mutation with evidence grading.

## Eligibility — included

Population/Condition/Context/Design: Nigeria-conducted or Nigeria-extractable data; lab-confirmed AIV (molecular and/or stated serological assay); ≥1 of epi/prevalence/outbreak/surveillance, molecular/phylogenetic, spatial/temporal, wild-bird finding; primary research, surveillance, outbreak investigation, molecular epidemiology with extractable data. Report traits: data in window; English full text (or abstract + translatable full text, method logged); peer-reviewed + defined grey (theses, WOAH/FDL reports) with sensitivity analysis. Any avian species if Nigeria-linked.

## Eligibility — excluded

Entirely outside Nigeria without Nigeria data; non-AIV only; no extractable epi/molecular/spatial-temporal data; editorials/commentaries/news/abstracts-without-data; lab-only experiments without field relevance; duplicate datasets (keep most comprehensive as primary, link companions).

## Selection process

Dedupe by tool + manual title/DOI check (auto vs manual counts for flow). Pilot 30–50 abstracts to calibrate. Two independent reviewers at title/abstract and full-text, blinded; conflicts by discussion then third-reviewer arbitration. One exclusion reason per full-text record (fixed list). Translation by stated method; authors contacted for unclear data. PRISMA 2020 flow diagram.

## Extraction

Piloted form (3–5 diverse studies, re-pilot if major change). Fields: biblio, design, period, state/zone, host/system, n, assay, subtype, clade/lineage, sequencing + phylogeny method/support + accession, reassortment/mutation claim, spatial/temporal finding, funding. Dual extraction for outcome fields; single + verification for descriptive fields. No blanks (Not reported/Unclear/N-A). Disagreements by discussion → arbiter → author contact.

## Risk of bias

Two appraisers, study-level, verbatim quotes; overall Include/Exclude/Seek. No score-sums. Feeds synthesis ordering + sensitivity.

## Synthesis
